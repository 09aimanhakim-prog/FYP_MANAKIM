Setup: enter measured field dimensions, place the robot at the center of the starting edge facing the goal, keep it SAFE, press Set current pose as start, then arm the desired mode. That button also makes the current physical direction gyro heading 0°. The map integrates RPM1–RPM3 and gyro heading at each STATUS update.

Ball calibration color
Select a color and start a calibration.

How to use: put the robot in its normal lighting, choose the ball color, and press Start. Click the literal center of the ball, then hold it still for about one second. Move the ball to a different distance or brighter/darker part of the floor and click again. Three samples are recommended; then press Save & apply.

Only a small patch around each click is analyzed. The robot stays SAFE, and the resulting HSV settings are saved in ball_colors.json. This does not train a neural network and adds almost no load during normal tracking.

Goal calibration color
Select a goal color and start calibration.

How to use: choose the goal color and press Start. Drag a box completely inside the solid colored goal area—avoid posts, floor, and walls. Hold still for about one second. Repeat from two or three positions or lighting conditions, then press Save & apply.

Goal HSV settings are independent from ball HSV settings and are saved in goal_colors.json. Geometry filtering then requires a large, wide region with left, right, and bottom edge support.
